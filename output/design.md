# Campus Customs — Design Refresh (Problem 10)

Styles live in `frontend/src/theme.css`, loaded after `index.css`. The artwork is drawn in code as inline SVG, with no image files: `HandsomeDan.tsx` and `NewHavenIcons.tsx`.

| What I changed | Why it should help customers stay and buy |
|---|---|
| **Palette: navy, white, and light blue only.** Navy `#0b2a4f` for text, buttons, and the header. White cards. Light blue `#e8f1fa` / `#a9c7e8` for page bands, the chat, and hover states. | Reads as official Yale and trustworthy. Every product photo sits on white, so the merch, not the page, stands out. |
| **Silver-and-gold metallic accents** (one gradient). Used only on thin details: the line under the announcement bar, the underline on the current nav tab, a stripe on page headers and section titles, the "Shop the collection" button, and the ring around Handsome Dan. | Adds a premium feel without being loud. It's rationed, so the eye goes to where it matters: the page you're on and the main button. |
| **Handsome Dan**, a cartoon English bulldog in a navy "Y" sweater with a silver-gold collar tag. He appears in the hero, the logo, the chat button ("Ask Dan"), the chat header, the footer, and the 404 page. | Gives the shop a friendly mascot and personality, and makes the brand memorable. Seeing his face on the chat button tells shoppers who to ask. |
| **Fonts:** *Fraunces* (a warm, slightly playful serif) for headings and *Inter* (a clean sans) for body text, prices, and forms. | Headings feel collegiate and fun, while prices, sizes, and forms stay crisp and easy to read. Professional, not gimmicky. |
| **New Haven / New England touches:**<br>- a charred-crust **apizza slice** and **maple leaves** floating gently around Dan in the hero;<br>- a **shoreline wave** edge on the hero and footer;<br>- a "New Haven favorites" row, where each item is a link: **Apizza-approved comfort** (hoodies), **Fall on the Green** (quarter-zips), **Shoreline layers** (jackets);<br>- a footer line: "Proudly New Haven, Connecticut". | Local pride makes it feel like *the* Yale shop, not a generic merch site. Each doodle also leads to a product category, so the charm doubles as navigation. |
| **The chat blends in.** The launcher is a slim navy pill: Dan's face + "Ask Dan · Sizes, prices & stock". The panel is white with a navy header, "Campus Customs Concierge · Live prices & stock from our shop floor", calm white and navy bubbles, and a thin metallic line. | Easy to find (always bottom-right, labeled) but it doesn't compete with product photos. The "concierge" wording and restrained styling tell shoppers it's a serious, reliable tool. |
| **Easier-to-see headers and navigation.** Nav tabs are larger. The current page gets a light-blue pill plus a metallic underline. Page titles (Products, About, …) sit in a light-blue header band with a metallic edge. Section titles have a short metallic underline. | Shoppers always know where they are and what's clickable, which means less getting lost and more browsing. |
| **Motion kept subtle.** Only the leaves and pizza drift, slowly, and they stop for users who set "reduce motion". Cards lift slightly on hover. | Lively without distracting from shopping. Respects accessibility settings. |
